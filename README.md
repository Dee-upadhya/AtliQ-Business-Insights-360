
The idea behind this project was to bring different areas of the business into one place — instead of looking at sales, finance, marketing and supply chain data separately.

I worked with multiple data sources and used Power BI to create an interactive report where users can filter the data by market, customer, product and time period.

1. Finance View

This view focuses on the company's financial performance, mainly **Net Sales, Gross Margin and Net Profit**.

What I like about this view is that we can look at the P&L at different levels — for example, by region, market or customer — and also compare the performance with benchmarks and previous years.

2. Sales View

Here, I can analyse performance across products, customers and markets.

The takeaway from this view is simple: **it helps the sales team understand not just where we are selling more, but where the sales are actually valuable to the business.**

3. Marketing View

Here, I can compare Net Sales, Gross Margin and Net Profit across different product levels.

The profitability and growth matrix makes it easier to spot products that are growing, products that are profitable, and areas that may need more attention.

So the main takeaway is that **marketing decisions can be based on actual product and market performance rather than just overall sales numbers.**

4. Supply Chain View

The **Supply Chain View** looks at a different but very important part of the business — forecasting.

Here, we have **forecast accuracy, net error and absolute error**, along with product and customer-level analysis.

The key takeaway from this view is **better forecasting can help the business maintain the right inventory levels while reducing supply-related risks.**

5. Executive View

This view is useful because an executive doesn't necessarily need to go through every detailed chart. They can start here, identify an area that needs attention and then move into the relevant detailed view.

The takeaway is that **the dashboard connects high-level business performance with the underlying details needed to investigate it.**

Overall, the main objective of this project was not just to create attractive Power BI visuals.

It was to make the data **interactive, easier to explore and more useful for decision-making**.

By bringing Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain and Executive insights together, the dashboard provides a more complete picture of business performance and helps move from **data to insight, and from insight to action.**
